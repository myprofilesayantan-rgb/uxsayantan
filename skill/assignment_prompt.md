# Analyttica Product Design Lead Assignment Prompt

## Objective
Every BI tool - Power BI, Looker, Qlik, Metabase, Tableau - makes a compromise between simplicity and power. Study how the leading tools handle this tension, then build a focused dashboarding tool built for a business user with no technical background. You may limit your study to Looker and Power BI (or another business-user-facing tool).

The hard part is deciding what to expose and what to hide, which chart types/parameters earn their place, how filtering and customization work without leaking technical concepts, and where an agentic / natural-language layer replaces manual configuration. We want to see the judgment behind those calls.

---

## Requirements
At minimum, a user must be able to:
1. **Add datasets**
2. **Build charts**
3. **Add filters**
4. **Add calculated fields**

How you implement each and what else you implement is your decision. You can't build everything in a week - so prioritise heavily.
We want evidence that you can reason about a user, make sharp design decisions, and ship something polished - even if that's a small, well-chosen subset done exceptionally well.

---

## Deliverables
1. **Working prototype** - an interactive prototyped app. Build it with AI tools; that's expected (they recommend Lovable or Claude Design, but vanilla HTML/CSS/JS is approved).
2. **1 page writeup** (optional)
3. **Resume**

---

## Evaluation Criteria
* **Prioritization**: Given a week, did you make smart calls about what to build, simplify, and cut — and can you justify them?
* **Abstraction**: How well is complexity hidden without removing power?
* **Visual craft**: Does the interface feel intentional, clean, and appropriate for the user?
* **AI fluency**: Did you direct AI tools well to move faster and produce better output?

---

## Constraints
* Built for a business user, not an analyst.
* Using AI tools is expected. We're not testing whether you can build without them — we're evaluating whether you can direct them well, judge what they produce, and move faster because of them.
